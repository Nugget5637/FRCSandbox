# REEFSCAPE (2025) pack: sources and assumptions

`pack.json` is generated by [`generate.mjs`](generate.mjs). Edit the generator, not the JSON, then run `npm run packs:reefscape`. Every measurement in the generator names its source.

Based on the 2025 Game Manual as published on frcmanual.com, checked November 13, 2026. That site does not say which Team Update its text includes, so the pack should be rechecked against the final official PDF and the 2025 Team Updates before it is treated as final.

## Verified against the manual

- Field 17.548 × 8.052 m (WPILib welded layout; manual 57 ft 6⅞ in × 26 ft 5 in), 20 in guardrails
- Reef: 65.5 in hexagon face to face, centered across the field; center 4.489 m from the blue wall from the AprilTags (manual says the reef is 12 ft from the alliance wall, which matches the near face, not the center)
- Trough (L1) front edge 18 in; pipes 13 in apart on each face, 1¼ in Schedule 40 (1.66 in OD)
- Branches: L2 and L3 angled up 35°, highest points 31⅞ in and 47⅝ in; L4 vertical, top at 72 in
- Barge: 44 in deep, horizontal truss 62 in above the carpet; cages 24 in tall and 7⅜ in wide; net 12 ft long with its low point 76 in up
- Processor opening 28 in wide, 20 in tall, 7 in above the carpet
- Coral station opening bottom 37.5 in, 7 in tall, 55° chute
- Coral: 11⅞ in long, 4½ in outside diameter, 0.5–0.8 kg. Algae: 16 in diameter
- Staging: 126 coral (1 on each of 6 marks, 1 preload per robot, the rest split behind the coral stations) and 18 algae (6 on each reef, 1 on each marked coral)
- Match: AUTO 15 s, TELEOP 135 s
- Points (Table 6-2): coral L1 3/2, L2 4/3, L3 6/4, L4 7/5 (AUTO/TELEOP); algae processor 6, net 4
- One coral per branch (6.5.1: the branch end must be inside the coral)
- Robot rules: perimeter ≤ 120 in, starting height ≤ 3 ft 6 in, extension ≤ 1 ft 6 in, weight ≤ 115 lb (R103-R105 and the inspection checklist agree)

## Problems found

- **Cage heights in manual 5.4.1.** The text says cages hang "at shallow or deep positions … such that the bottom of the cage is 3½ in. and 2 ft. 5⅜ in. from the carpet respectively", which reads as shallow = 3½ in. Deep cages hang on extra chain, so they are the low ones, and 6328's measured values agree (deep 3.125 in, shallow 30.125 in). The pack uses 3½ in for the deep cage.
- **Reef distance.** Manual 5.3 says each reef is "located 12 ft. away from the ALLIANCE WALL". The near face (tag 18) is 3.658 m = 12.0 ft from the wall, so this is the near face, not the center. The pack uses the AprilTag center.
- **frcmanual.com revision.** The site does not state which Team Update its text reflects.

## Not modeled yet

- **Cage climbing and parking** (shallow 6, deep 12, park 2) and **LEAVE** (3 points in AUTO). Need robot-state scoring and the bot creator's mechanisms.
- **Ranking points and the Coopertition bonus** (AUTO RP, CORAL RP, BARGE RP; 2 algae in each processor). Need threshold bonuses.
- **Fouls** and position rules, such as G412 (throw coral only from the reef zone) and G421 (one defender at a time).
- **Contact-based scoring**: coral counts only while no robot or algae touches it, AUTO points come off if a coral is knocked off a branch, and trough coral can be supported by other coral. Scored pieces leave the field in this engine, so a scored coral disappears instead of staying on its branch.
- **Processor to human player**: algae through the processor goes to the opponent's human player, who can throw it into their net. Algae scored in the processor simply leaves play here.
- **3-second assessment** after AUTO and TELEOP.
- **Real swerve** and robot tipping (Milestone 4).
